03 — Papers & ongoing work
First-authored · ICONICA

Emergent Modularity in Transformer Attention

Siddhartha Shankar, Nikhil Kore, Soumyalatha Naveen — MIT Bengaluru, MAHE

A large-scale empirical test of the additivity assumption in mechanistic interpretability — the common but rarely-verified claim that ablating multiple attention heads produces a performance drop equal to the sum of their individual drops. Pairwise ablation across four transformer models and two benchmark tasks shows that head-level modularity is an emergent property of scale rather than something built into the architecture: small models exhibit substantial non-additive interaction, while models above roughly 300M parameters behave close to additively.
